Install Expense Tracker on Phone

Works offline on Android & iOS Home Screen

Ready for 1-Click Android / Chrome Installation!

🤖 Android (Chrome / Edge / Samsung Internet)

  1. Open your hosted GitHub Pages link in Google Chrome on Android.
  2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
  3. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen" and tap Install.
  4. Expense Tracker will appear in your app drawer and home screen like a native Android app!

🍎 iPhone & iPad (iOS Safari)

  1. Open your hosted GitHub Pages link in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Share button ↑ at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner — it launches in full-screen standalone mode without browser bars!
